# V10 Phase 3 — Teams, Rivals & Matchups

Phase 3 adds social/tactical systems on top of V10 characters.

## Teams
- Create named squads with descriptions.
- Add up to four of your own or public shinobi.
- Analyze squad rating, cohesion, coverage and leadership.
- Generate suggested tactical roles for each member.

## Rivals
- Declare a public shinobi as a rival of one of your characters.
- Save an optional rivalry note.
- Jump directly from a rivalry into matchup analysis.

## Matchups
- Compare your own and public shinobi.
- Uses derived stats, chakra relationships, summons and profile traits.
- Shows projected edge, factor breakdown, counters and win conditions.
- Saves matchup history to the signed-in account.

Matchup projections are intentionally profile-based and should not be treated as canon power rankings.
