# V9 Rank Potential Hotfix

This patch expands the Rank Potential assessment so the upper end of the ladder is actually reachable.

## Rank ladder

- Genin Potential
- Chūnin Potential
- Special Jōnin Potential
- Jōnin Potential
- Elite Jōnin Potential
- Kage Candidate
- Kage Potential
- Legendary Potential

## Balance changes

Kage Potential now rewards consistently strong answers around village-scale judgment, leadership, accountability, adaptability, uncertainty, and responsibility for other people.

Legendary Potential is intentionally much rarer. It requires a profile that repeatedly selects the most demanding responsibility/leadership answers, with especially strong weighting around failure ownership, village-wide impact, legacy, and crisis leadership.

The existing Kage Candidate result remains intact for compatibility with previously saved V9 characters.

## Validation

The quiz-data tests now confirm that every Rank Potential question can contribute toward both Kage Potential and Legendary Potential while keeping the four-answer V9 quiz contract.
